# Coffee Shop Sales Analytics 

## Overview
An interactive sales analytics dashboard and SQL/BI reporting breakdown tracking coffee shop performance across store locations, product categories, day types (weekday vs. weekend), and hourly traffic.

## Key Highlights 
- **Total Sales:** $76K (-6.8% / -$5.5K vs. Last Month)
- **Total Orders:** 16,359 (-5.5% / -1.2K vs. Last Month)
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 23,550 (-5.3% / -1.3K vs. Last Month)
- **Daily Average Sales:** $2,719 with peaks reaching $3.2K
- **Weekday vs. Weekend Split:** Weekdays generated $54K (70.92%) vs. $22K (~29.08%) on weekends
- **Store Performance:** Lower Manhattan ($25.32K, -4.6% vs. LM), Hell's Kitchen ($25.72K, -7.6% vs. LM), and Astoria ($25.11K, -8.1% vs. LM)
- **Peak Traffic Window:** Morning rush between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM (peaking at $10K in the 10:00 AM slot)

## Top Categories & Items
- **Main Categories:** Coffee ($29.27K), Tea ($21.73K), Bakery ($9.04K), Drinking Chocolate ($8.13K)
- **Notable Product Breakdowns:** Barista Espresso ($10.03K, -4.1%), Brewed Chai tea ($8.38K, -4.3%), Hot chocolate ($8.13K, -2.5%), Gourmet brewed coffee ($7.65K, -5.2%)

## Tools & Skills Demonstrated
- Dashboard design & interactive filtering (Month/Year slicer, calendar selector, cross-filtering)
- KPI & variance analysis (MoM % change and absolute delta vs. Last Month)
- Time-series, weekday/weekend split, store comparison, and hourly heatmap analysis
